A recipe that recreates the taste of Gcova chicken at home using relatively affordable chicken. The sauce tastes just like real commercial Korean fried chicken sauce!

![Gcova Chicken](/images/posts/gcova_chicken_4.png)

## 1. Ingredients

![Chicken](/images/posts/gcova_chicken_1.png)

I used boneless chicken tendorloins purchased from Amazon. However, any other chicken parts work perfectly fine.

- **Main Ingredients**: Chicken (thighs or breast, etc.) 1 pack, cooking oil, rice cakes for tteokbokki, 2 cheongyang chili peppers, sesame seeds
- **Sauce**: 3 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp red pepper powder, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 5 tbsp corn syrup, 1 tbsp ketchup (or 1/2 tbsp vinegar + 1/2 tbsp sugar), 1 tbsp gochujang (Korean red pepper paste), 1 tbsp minced garlic
- **Cooking Tools**: Pan, measuring cup, cooking tongs

## 2. Cooking Steps

### 1) Grilling the Chicken
Add oil to the pan, add the chicken, and cook over medium heat (5-6 on induction). At this point, cutting the chicken into smaller pieces helps it cook through and makes it easier to eat in one bite.

![Grilled Chicken](/images/posts/gcova_chicken_2.png)

### 2) Making the Sauce
Mix all the sauce ingredients in a bowl.

### 3) Adding Sauce and Cooking
Add the sauce to the pan with the cooking chicken. Then add a handful of rice cakes, slice 1 cheongyang chili pepper diagonally and add it, and cook. Finally, add sesame seeds and cook.
